**Long Description**
Anger can feel overwhelming, but it doesn't have to be. Ember is a private, supportive space designed to help you notice, understand, and release anger before it takes over.

Start with a quick tension check-in. Slide from calm to boiling over and read a grounding message made for that moment. From there, choose your release: slow your breath with a guided breathing exercise, speak freely into the voice vent, write and shred a thought in the journal, or take a mindful walk to reset.

Over time, Face Check and your check-in history build a picture of your emotional patterns — not to judge, but to help you feel more in control. With light, dark, and system themes, Ember fits the mood you're in.

Whether you're looking for anger management, stress relief, emotion tracking, or simple breathing exercises, Ember meets you where you are and helps you move forward calmly.
